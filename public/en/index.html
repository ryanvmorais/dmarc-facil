Can someone send email on behalf of your domain?

Type the domain and see what DMARC, SPF and DKIM say about it and what to do now.

The lookup leaves your browser and goes straight to Cloudflare's public DNS (or Google's, if Cloudflare does not answer). Whoever answers sees the domain, the DKIM selectors we try and your IP. DMARC Fácil does not store the domain you type.

What each record does

The three work together so your email cannot be spoofed.

DMARC

Tells the receiving provider what to do with email that pretends to be your domain: nothing (p=none), send it to spam (p=quarantine) or reject it (p=reject). Start at p=none and go up one step at a time.

SPF

Lists who may send email for your domain. The limit is 10 DNS lookups: going over it invalidates the record, which is why the page counts each one.

DKIM

Signs each message with a key. The selector is the sender's choice and DNS does not let anyone list them, so the page searches the most common ones and says “not found in these selectors”, never “you don't have DKIM”.
